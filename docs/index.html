I am an assistant professor in Graduate School of AI and Computer Science & Engineering at POSTECH. Before joining POSTECH, I was an associate professor in AI Graduate School at GIST and a Researcher at the Intelligent Convergence Research Laboratory, ETRI. I had been a postdoctoral researcher in the Computer Vision Lab. at the Seoul National University. I received my Ph.D. degree from POSTECH, under the supervision of Prof. Bohyung Han and Minsu Cho, and B.S. and M.S degrees from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Ewha Womans University.

My research interests lie in the intersection of computer vision, machine learning and deep learning, which include visual tracking, object detection, segmentation, and learning with minimal or no supervision by human. I am also particularly interested in trustworthy and robust AI, with a focus on combating deepfakes, protecting visual data from malicious manipulation, and building reliable vision systems that can be safely deployed in real-world environments.
